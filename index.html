Who we are

A community-led beginning.

BAGS Collective is a community-based organisation established in 2026. We are at the beginning of our journey: building relationships, listening to neighbours and creating space for people to work together on shared priorities.

Our purpose is simple: help make it easier for people to connect, contribute and shape positive change in their community. We believe everyone has something valuable to bring.
